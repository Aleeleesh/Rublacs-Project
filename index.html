05 / RESEARCH

Our research journey

We used our experiences, guides, and worksheets to think about what type of streaming content could work best for our audience.

RESEARCH STEP 01

Identify the Audience

We first considered who would actually be interested in watching our Roblox stream. This helped us focus the concept on viewers who already enjoy gaming, Roblox, and interactive entertainment.

Audience Teenagers & Roblox players
Interest Gaming & entertainment
RESEARCH STEP 02

Explore Content

We considered different Roblox experiences and thought about which ones could provide interesting gameplay, reactions, challenges, exploration, and opportunities for interaction.

Variety Multiple Roblox experiences
Goal Keep gameplay engaging
RESEARCH STEP 03

Understand Viewer Needs

We looked at what viewers might need from a stream: entertainment, interaction, consistency, and clear communication. These ideas influenced the features included in our website concept.

Focus Viewer experience
Result More engaging content
RESEARCH STEP 04

Develop the Stream

We combined our findings into a concept that includes gameplay, commentary, reactions, viewer interaction, and different types of Roblox content.

Concept Interactive Roblox stream
Style Fun, social, engaging
RESEARCH STEP 05

Reflect & Improve

Research is not only about creating the first idea. We can continue improving the stream by observing what works, listening to viewers, and adjusting our content and presentation.

Improve Based on audience feedback
Goal Better viewer experience

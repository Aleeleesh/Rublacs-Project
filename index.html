OUR RESEARCH

Why Roblox works for our project

Our research helped us decide what kind of streaming content would fit our group and our intended audience.

01
STREAMING CONTENT

Why Roblox?

Roblox is more than one game. Players can explore a wide range of user-created experiences with different goals, styles, challenges, and ways to play.

For our stream, that variety gives us room to explore different games, try challenges, react naturally, and give viewers choices about what happens next.

This makes Roblox useful for a streaming project because gameplay does not have to follow only one format.

02
TARGET AUDIENCE

Teen Roblox Viewers

Our target audience is people aged 13 and up, especially teenagers who enjoy playing or watching Roblox.

We want the stream to feel fun, engaging, easy to follow, and welcoming to viewers who already enjoy gaming.

The idea also works well for viewers who like watching friends play, reacting to funny moments, and helping decide what happens next.

03
VIEWER NEEDS

What do viewers want?

Viewers need fun content, clear gameplay, involvement, and genuine interaction.

Gameplay should be entertaining instead of repetitive, commentary should make the stream easy to follow, and viewers should have opportunities to participate.

Comments, questions, polls, and suggestions can help viewers feel like part of the stream.

04
GOOD STREAM

What makes a stream good?

A good stream should keep viewers interested while making them feel welcome and involved.

Clear audio and video make the stream easier to watch. Interesting content gives viewers a reason to stay.

Genuine interaction and a friendly attitude also make the experience feel more natural and enjoyable.

05

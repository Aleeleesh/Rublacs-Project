05 / GOOD STREAM

What makes a stream good?

Click each category to explore the role it plays in a livestream.

Commentary can explain what is happening, react to unexpected moments, and keep the stream interesting even when gameplay slows down.

Reading chat, answering questions, using polls, and reacting to viewer suggestions can turn passive viewers into participants.

A stream can have a simple goal such as exploring a game, completing a challenge, testing something new, or playing with friends.

Good moderation, respectful communication, privacy awareness, and age-appropriate behavior help make the community safer and more enjoyable.

🧠
DIGITAL AWARENESS

Recommendation algorithms need responsibility too.

Recommendation systems can help people discover content, but repeatedly showing similar or increasingly attention-grabbing content can narrow what someone sees. Viewers should still think critically about what they watch and how much time they spend online.

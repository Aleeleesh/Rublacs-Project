01 • RESEARCH

Why Roblox streaming?

We looked at what makes gaming content enjoyable and what viewers actually need from a stream.

01

Streaming Content

Our stream focuses on playing different Roblox experiences while keeping the audience involved throughout the stream.

ExploreDiscover different Roblox experiences.
ChallengeTake on fun challenges during streams.
ReactShare genuine reactions and commentary.
ConnectRead comments and viewer suggestions.
02

Target Audience

Our target audience is people aged 13 and up, especially teenagers who enjoy playing or watching Roblox.

13+ Teen Roblox Viewers

Viewer Needs

  • Fun Content — gameplay should not feel boring or repetitive.
  • Easy to Follow — clear gameplay and commentary.
  • Involvement — viewers can comment, ask questions, and suggest ideas.
02 • RESEARCH JOURNEY

How we got here

01

Reflect

We thought about our own interests, experiences, and the games we already know how to play.

02

Choose

We realized gaming, especially Roblox, would be a good choice because we already know the game and enjoy it.

03

Plan

We decided to combine gameplay with challenges, reactions, commentary, and viewer interaction.

WHAT WE LEARNED

Good streaming is more than just gameplay.

Our research helped us understand that viewers want entertainment, interaction, and content that is easy to follow. Roblox gives us many different experiences to work with, which makes the format flexible.

03 • PLATFORM & EQUIPMENT

What a stream needs

01

Streaming Platform

We designed our concept around a live-streaming experience where viewers can watch gameplay and interact through chat.

02

Computer

A computer is used to run Roblox, manage the stream, and handle other streaming software.

03

Microphone

Clear audio helps viewers understand commentary, reactions, and conversations during gameplay.

04

Camera / Screen Capture

Screen capture lets the audience see the Roblox gameplay while the streamer interacts with chat.

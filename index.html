RESEARCH

Why Roblox works for streaming.

Our research connects Roblox, our intended viewers, and the characteristics that make a stream interesting and interactive.

01
STREAMING TOPIC / CONTENT

Why Roblox?

Roblox gives players access to a huge variety of user-created experiences. This gives our stream many possibilities for gameplay, challenges, reactions, and audience interaction.

Roblox is more than one game. Players can explore obstacle courses, simulators, role-playing experiences, adventure games, and many other types of content. This variety makes it easier to change the direction of a stream without leaving the platform.

02
TARGET AUDIENCE

Teen Roblox Viewers

Our target audience is people aged 13 and up, especially teenagers who enjoy playing or watching Roblox.

We want the stream to feel fun, engaging, and easy to enjoy. Viewers should understand what is happening without needing complicated explanations, while still having opportunities to participate.

03
VIEWER NEEDS

What viewers need

Viewers need entertaining content, clear gameplay, and ways to feel involved with what is happening.

Fun Content Easy to Follow Involvement Interaction

A stream should not just show gameplay. Viewers should have reasons to keep watching and opportunities to communicate, vote, suggest ideas, and react to moments in the stream.

04
CHARACTERISTICS

What makes a good stream?

A good stream should keep viewers interested while making them feel welcome and involved.

✓ Clear audio & video
✓ Interesting content
✓ Genuine interaction
✓ Friendly & attentive

These characteristics help a stream become easier to follow and more enjoyable. Interaction is especially important because viewers should feel like they are participating instead of simply watching.

05
OUR RESEARCH JOURNEY

From an idea to BlockLive

Our worksheets, guides, and background research helped us connect our interests with the needs of our intended audience.

01 Reflect We thought about our interests, experiences, and games we already know.
02 Choose We realized gaming, especially Roblox, was a strong choice because we already know and enjoy it.
03 Plan We combined gameplay with challenges, reactions, commentary, and interaction.

Through the research process, we learned that our topic should connect with both what we enjoy and what viewers need. Roblox gave us enough variety to build a stream concept that could stay interactive and entertaining.

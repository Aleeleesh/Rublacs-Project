04 / VIEWER NEEDS

What does the audience need?

A successful stream should provide more than gameplay. It should give viewers a reason to stay involved.

05 / GOOD STREAM

What makes a stream good?

Click each category to explore it.

Commentary can explain what is happening, react to unexpected moments and keep the stream interesting when gameplay slows down.

Reading chat, answering questions, using polls and reacting to suggestions can turn viewers into participants.

A stream can have a simple goal such as exploring a game, completing a challenge or testing something new.

Good moderation, respectful communication, privacy awareness and age-appropriate behavior help create a better community.

🧠
DIGITAL AWARENESS

Recommendation algorithms need responsibility too.

Recommendation systems can help people discover content, but repeatedly showing similar or increasingly attention-grabbing content can narrow what someone sees. Viewers should still think critically about what they watch and how much time they spend online.

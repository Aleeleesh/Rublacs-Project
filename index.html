PROJECT RESEARCH

Understanding live streaming

01

What is live streaming?

Live streaming broadcasts video over the internet while viewers watch and interact with the content in real time.

Unlike a normal uploaded video, live streams happen while the audience is watching. Features such as chat, reactions, and polls allow viewers to participate during the broadcast.
02

Why Roblox?

Roblox offers thousands of different experiences, making it a useful platform for exploring interactive streaming.

Roblox includes many genres, from roleplay and obstacle courses to simulators and adventure games. This variety gives streamers different types of content to share.
03

Why does chat matter?

Real-time chat gives viewers a way to communicate with the streamer and other people watching.

Interaction can make a stream feel more like a shared experience. Viewers can react to gameplay, suggest ideas, and take part in community activities.

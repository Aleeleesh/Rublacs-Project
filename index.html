OUR RESEARCH

Why Roblox?

Our research helped us connect the type of content we enjoy with what viewers may want from a gaming stream.

01

Streaming Topic / Content

Our streaming topic is Roblox gameplay. We want to explore different Roblox experiences while including challenges, reactions, commentary, and interaction with viewers.

Roblox gives us flexibility because there are many different experiences and styles of gameplay. This allows our stream to change depending on what the audience finds interesting.

02

Target Audience

Our target audience is people aged 13 and up, especially teenagers who enjoy playing or watching Roblox.

We want the stream to feel fun, welcoming, and easy to follow. Viewers should be able to enjoy the gameplay while also having opportunities to participate.

03

Viewer Needs

Viewers need fun content, clear gameplay, easy-to-follow commentary, and opportunities to become involved.

Comments, questions, suggestions, and polls can make viewers feel like they are part of the stream instead of only watching it.

04

Characteristics of a Good Stream

A good stream should have clear audio and video, interesting content, genuine interaction, and a friendly attitude.

We also found that consistency and audience interaction can help keep viewers interested throughout a stream.

05

Our Research Journey

We started by thinking about our interests and experiences, considered different streaming topics, and eventually chose Roblox because it was something we already knew and enjoyed.

01 Explore Look at different types of streaming content.
02 Reflect Think about our interests and existing knowledge.
03 Choose Select Roblox as our main streaming topic.
04 Plan Combine gameplay, challenges, reactions, and interaction.

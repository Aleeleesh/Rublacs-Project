OUR PROJECT

Research & Planning

The ideas that helped us decide what our stream should be and how viewers should experience it.

05 CORE
SECTIONS
01
STREAMING TOPIC / CONTENT

Why Roblox?

Our stream will be all about Roblox gameplay, where we can jump into different games, take on fun challenges, explore new experiences, and chat with viewers along the way. We can also add our own commentary, reactions, and a few spontaneous moments to make the stream feel more lively and enjoyable for our viewers.

Roblox is an online globally available game that can be played by everyone. Instead of a single title, Roblox functions as a massive library of user-created experiences ranging from obstacle courses and simulators to role-playing games, and many other experiences.

You can also create your own game through Roblox Studio; it allows you to code, build, and publish your own interactive 3D worlds for other players to enjoy.

Another fascinating thing you can do on Roblox is designing your own Avatar with hairs, faces, bodies, accessories, and clothes. These items can be free while some can be bought using Robux.

Players can join sessions with friends, chat via available text features, or use voice features while exploring virtual games together.

Roblox also has safety systems and content controls intended to help protect younger players.

02
TARGET AUDIENCE

Who are we streaming for?

Our target audience is people aged 13 and up, especially teenagers who enjoy playing or watching Roblox.

We want our stream to feel fun, engaging, and like something they can enjoy with the people they are closest to.

The audience is important to our concept because Roblox can be experienced socially. Our stream focuses on making viewers feel like they are participating instead of only watching.

03
VIEWER NEEDS

What does the audience want?

The viewers might want a stream that feels fun, interesting, and easy to follow, which is exactly what we will be doing.

They might also want to feel included, so we will interact with them by reading comments, answering questions, and listening to their suggestions.

This is why the website includes a live chat, game selection, and poll. These features represent the type of interaction we want our stream to have.

04
CHARACTERISTICS OF A GOOD STREAM

What makes a good stream?

A good stream should feel welcoming and engaging to the viewers, with clear audio and video, interesting content, and genuine interaction with them.

The streamer(s) should also come across as energetic, friendly, and attentive, making people feel more involved while keeping the conversation lively and the audience interested.

Good streaming is not only about playing a game. Communication and interaction are also important parts of making viewers want to stay involved.

05
OUR RESEARCH JOURNEY

How did we decide?

Our experience guides and worksheets helped us figure out what type of streaming content would work best for us.

Through the activities, we looked at different kinds of streams, thought about our interests and experiences, and eventually realized that gaming, especially Roblox, would be a good choice for us.

We already know how to play it and think it would be fun to share that experience with viewers. That made Roblox a natural choice for our project.

OUR RESEARCH / 02

Why Roblox streaming?

Our research helped us connect our own interests with the needs of the audience we wanted to reach.

01

STREAMING TOPIC / CONTENT

Roblox Gameplay

Our stream focuses on playing different Roblox experiences while keeping the audience involved throughout the stream.

Roblox gives us a wide range of experiences to choose from. Instead of focusing on one type of gameplay, our concept can move between exploration, challenges, reactions, and interaction with viewers.

  • Explore different experiences
  • Take on challenges
  • React naturally to gameplay
  • Connect with the audience
02

TARGET AUDIENCE

Teen Roblox Viewers

Our target audience is people aged 13 and up, especially teenagers who enjoy playing or watching Roblox.

We want the experience to feel approachable and entertaining for viewers who already understand gaming culture while still being easy for someone new to follow.

03

VIEWER NEEDS

Fun, Clear & Interactive

Viewers need content that is entertaining, easy to follow, and gives them opportunities to participate.

  • Fun content
  • Easy-to-follow gameplay
  • Audience involvement
  • Genuine interaction
04

GOOD STREAM

What Makes It Good?

A good stream should keep viewers interested while making them feel welcome and involved.

  • Clear audio and video
  • Interesting content
  • Genuine interaction
  • Friendly and attentive presenters
05

04
Learn

Our worksheets, guides, and background research helped us connect our ideas with viewer needs.

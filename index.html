What is Roblox?

Roblox is an online platform that is globally available and can be played by people around the world. Instead of being a single game, Roblox functions as a massive library of user-created experiences, ranging from obstacle courses and simulators to role-playing games and many more.

Players can also create their own games using Roblox Studio. It allows creators to code, build, and publish interactive 3D worlds for other players to enjoy. Creators can earn real money by designing popular games or selling virtual goods to other users.

👤 Avatar Customization

Players can design their own Avatar with different hairstyles, faces, bodies, accessories, and clothes. Some items are free, while others can be bought using Robux.

💰 Robux

Robux is Roblox's virtual currency. It can be purchased with real money and used to buy avatar items or game upgrades such as special powers, tools, or VIP access inside individual games.

👥 Social Features

Players can join sessions with friends, communicate through text chat, and use voice features while exploring virtual experiences together.

🛡️ Player Safety

Roblox uses Maturity Content Filters to help protect younger players. These filters restrict access to experiences based on age ratings, such as All Ages, 9+, 13+, and 17+.

Roblox also applies additional restrictions for younger users and filters things such as numbers, links, and personal information to help keep younger players safer while using the platform.

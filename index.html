DOCUMENTATION

Research

The ideas behind the BlockLive project.

01

Why streaming?

Live streaming allows creators and viewers to interact in real time. It combines video, communication, entertainment, and community features.

A streaming platform needs more than video. Chat, reactions, viewer statistics, notifications, and moderation all contribute to the experience.

02

Recommendation systems

Recommendation algorithms can help viewers discover content they may enjoy, but they can also create problems when users are repeatedly shown similar content.

A responsible system should consider user choice, transparency, privacy, and the possibility of unhealthy recommendation loops.

03

Online communities

Chat systems make streams feel more social by allowing viewers to react and communicate while content is live.

Good moderation tools help keep communities useful, welcoming, and easier for creators to manage.
